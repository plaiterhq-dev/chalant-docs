Routine builder

The routine builder lets shoppers build a routine one step at a time, such as Cleanse, Treat and Protect, choosing one product for each. They see a running total and add the whole routine to their cart with one button.

Where you can use it

  • Any page, as a section. Add the Routine builder section, for example to your home page.
  • Product pages, as a block. In the Product information section, add the Routine builder block. When a shopper views a product that's part of a step, that product is chosen for them in its step.

Set up a routine

  1. In the theme editor, add the Routine builder section, or select the Routine builder block on a product page.
  2. Give the routine a Routine name, such as Morning routine. Shoppers see it in the cart next to each product from the routine.
  3. Add a Step block for each step. Give it a Step name, such as Cleanse, and an optional Description.
  4. In each step, choose the Products to choose from. Shoppers pick one of them.
  5. Turn on Optional step for steps shoppers can skip. They see a Skip this step choice and the total updates.
  6. Choose a Layout and change the Button label if you like.

Steps with no products are hidden from shoppers. A routine with no steps is hidden too, so you can set it up in the editor before anyone sees it.

In the cart

Each product from a routine is added as its own cart line, labeled with the routine name, for example Part of Morning routine. Shoppers can change quantities or remove items like any other line. If a product can't be added, for example because it just sold out, the shopper sees a message explaining why.

Products with several options, such as size, show a dropdown once chosen, so the shopper picks the exact variant.

Show the step on product cards

Product cards across your store can show which step a product belongs to, as a small label such as Cleanse.

  1. Add a tag to the product that starts with the step tag prefix, for example step:Cleanse.
  2. In Theme settings, Routines, check that Show routine step on product cards is on. Change the Step tag prefix if you already use step: for something else.

Ideas

  • A morning routine and an evening routine on the home page, as two sections.
  • A routine block on every serum's product page, suggesting the cleanser before it and the moisturizer after.
  • A gift set builder: Choose a scent, Choose a body lotion, Add a candle.
