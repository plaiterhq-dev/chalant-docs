Products

How Chalant shows product features that you set up in your Shopify admin.

Options and color swatches

Product options show as buttons or a dropdown. Choose the style in the Variant picker block. Options with no available variant stay selectable and are struck through, so shoppers can see what exists.

To show color swatches, set the product's category in the product page of your admin, then link the color option to the category's color metafield. Each value can have a color or an image. Chalant shows the swatch on the product page and in collection filters.

Images and video

Add images, videos and 3D models to a product in your admin. When a shopper picks a variant that has its own image, the gallery moves to it. Set a focal point on an image in your admin to keep the important part in view when it's cropped.

Subscriptions

Install a subscription app, such as Shopify Subscriptions, and add a subscription plan to a product. Chalant shows the plans under Purchase options, with the subscription price, and the cart shows the plan the shopper chose. Products that can only be bought on subscription preselect the first plan.

Gift cards

Create a gift card product in Products, Gift cards. On its product page, shoppers can choose to send it to someone else, with the recipient's email, name, a message and a send date. What they type is kept if they change the amount.

Pairs well with

Install Shopify's free Search and Discovery app and add complementary products to a product. In the theme editor, add the Complementary products block to the product page. Products without complementary products don't show the block.

The Related products section below the product page works on its own. Shopify chooses related products automatically.

Local pickup

Turn on local pickup for a location in Settings, Shipping and delivery. The Pickup availability block shows whether the product can be picked up and opens store details.

Filters and sorting

Install the Search and Discovery app to choose which filters appear on collection and search pages, such as availability, price, product type or color. Turn filtering and sorting on or off in the Collection and Search results sections, and choose whether filters show in a sidebar or a drawer on large screens.

Unit prices and Shop Pay Installments

Unit prices you set on a variant, such as $93.30 / 100 ml, show on product cards, the product page and in the cart. When Shop Pay Installments is turned on for your store, the installment message shows under the price on the product page.
